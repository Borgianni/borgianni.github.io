Luca Borgianni, Ph.D.

AI Systems Architect · Real-Time Distributed Intelligence

I design the next generation of AI infrastructure for real-time distributed intelligence. My research focuses on how multimodal AI systems coordinate computation, communication, and decision making across edge devices, cloud infrastructures, and future networks.

I bridge advanced R&D and academic research between the University of Pisa and Stanford University, building systems that execute complex inferences (from networked audio processing to biomedical detection) when bandwidth, latency, and energy are critical constraints.

Research Vision

"The next decade will not be limited by the size of our AI models. It will be limited by our ability to orchestrate billions of AI models distributed across heterogeneous infrastructures. My mission is to build the operating system of future AI: the runtime architectures that decide where, when, and how to execute intelligence across the edge-cloud continuum."

🌐 Distributed AI Systems

How can millions of AI agents coordinate in real time? Designing multi-agent architectures that distribute inference load seamlessly across constrained nodes.

⚡ AI Runtime Infrastructures

How should computation move across networks? Applying Reinforcement Learning (PPO) to orchestrate data streams, computing power, and energy dynamically.

🎙️ Real-Time Multimodal AI

Using live audio, speech, and video reconstruction at the Edge as the ultimate stress test for zero-latency communication and semantic representations.
