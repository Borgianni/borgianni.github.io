Luca Borgianni, Ph.D.

Building AI Systems that Think Beyond the Cloud

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Pisa and an Affiliate Researcher at Stanford University. I received my Ph.D. cum laude from the University of Pisa, which included a 6-month visiting fellowship at Stanford CCRMA. My research focuses on the systems challenges that will define the next generation of Artificial Intelligence. Rather than developing larger AI models, I investigate how distributed AI systems can efficiently coordinate computation, communication, and decision making across edge devices, cloud infrastructures, and future networks.

Currently, I am part of the Horizon Europe project EU-TRAINS, leading the design of distributed edge AI for sports applications. Concurrently, I collaborate with Stanford Medicine and CCRMA on AI-driven biomedical signal analysis. My work combines distributed systems, networking, machine learning, and real-time multimedia to design scalable AI infrastructures. I use applications such as speech processing, wearable sensing, satellite communications, and networked music performance as demanding real-world testbeds to develop architectures that make large-scale distributed intelligence practical, efficient, and reliable.

Research Vision

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly moving from centralized cloud models to billions of intelligent devices operating across heterogeneous environments.

The next decade will not be limited by larger foundation models, but by our ability to orchestrate computation, communication, and intelligence at planetary scale. My long-term research vision is to build the systems architecture that enables distributed AI to become reliable, efficient, secure, and real-time.
