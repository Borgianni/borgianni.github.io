Luca Borgianni, Ph.D.

Postdoctoral Researcher · Dept. of Information Engineering · University of Pisa

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Pisa and an Affiliate Researcher at Stanford University (CCRMA). My research thrives at the intersection of Resilient Data-Driven Networking for 6G, Low-Latency Edge AI, and Networked Music Performance using LEO Satellite Internet (Starlink).

Currently, I work within the Horizon Europe project "EU-TRAINS", developing Edge AI systems for swimming wearable assessment. Concurrently, I collaborate with Stanford Medicine School on the TAM project, implementing Machine Learning for automated tracheal breath sound analysis.

🏊🚴🏃 Competitive Sport & Triathlon
