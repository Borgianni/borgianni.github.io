Luca Borgianni, Ph.D.

Postdoctoral Researcher · Dept. of Information Engineering · University of Pisa

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Pisa and an Affiliate Researcher at Stanford University (CCRMA). My work is focused on Resilient Data-Driven Networking for 6G, Low-Latency Edge AI, and Networked Music Performance using LEO Satellite Internet (Starlink).

Currently, I lead Edge AI systems development and in-water communication architectures as WP8 Leader within the Horizon Europe project "EU-TRAINS". Concurrently, I collaborate with Stanford Medicine on real-time breath sound processing using Machine Learning (TAM project).
